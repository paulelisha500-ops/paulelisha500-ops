## Featured project

### [SkinSense](https://github.com/paulelisha500-ops/skinsense): AI skin screening, routines and progress tracking

Final-year team project (3 members), live on Hugging Face Spaces.

- A fine-tuned **EfficientNet-B0** classifies a skin photo across seven conditions: acne, dry skin, eczema, hyperpigmentation, normal, oily and rosacea
- Shows the confidence for every class and marks low-confidence results as unclear
- Builds morning and evening routines from a catalogue of 34 products
- Tracks check-ins, streaks and before/after photos over a 30-day plan

**Stack:** PyTorch, torchvision, OpenCV, Gradio, Hugging Face Hub
